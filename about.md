# About

This is a minimal technical blog focused on systems programming, software architecture, and the craft of building reliable software.

## Philosophy

Good technical writing should be:

- **Clear** — Ideas expressed without unnecessary complexity
- **Honest** — Including limitations and trade-offs
- **Practical** — Rooted in real-world experience

## Topics

Posts here cover:

- Systems programming and low-level details
- Software architecture and design patterns
- Development tooling and workflows
- Performance optimization
- Debugging and troubleshooting
